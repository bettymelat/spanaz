# Serene Home Spa

Create a premium, modern, trustworthy home-spa and massage website for SPA NAZ, a professional Ethiopian-owned home spa service operating in Bucharest, Romania.

The website must feel like a real premium wellness business, not a generic massage template. The main goal is to convert visitors into WhatsApp inquiries and bookings for home massage treatments in Bucharest.

1. BRAND

Business name: SPA NAZ

Business type: Professional home massage / home spa service

Location: Bucharest, Romania

Founder/background: Ethiopian

Positioning:
SPA NAZ brings a professional, relaxing spa experience directly to the customer's home. The brand should communicate warmth, professionalism, privacy, cleanliness, relaxation, trust, and personal care.

Do not make the website feel cheap, overly sexual, or like an amateur classified advertisement.

The brand should feel:

Elegant

Calm

Premium but accessible

Feminine and sophisticated

Warm and welcoming

Professional

Trustworthy

Personal

Relaxing

Use the Ethiopian background as part of the brand story in a tasteful and authentic way. Do not make the entire website look stereotypically Ethiopian. Instead, subtly incorporate warmth, cultural identity, hospitality, and natural elements.

2. MAIN WEBSITE GOAL

The primary conversion goal is:

“Book a relaxing massage at your home in Bucharest.”

A visitor should immediately understand:

What SPA NAZ is

That the massage is provided at their home

That the service is available in Bucharest

What treatments are available

How much they cost

How to book

Why they should trust SPA NAZ

Every important section should guide the visitor toward booking.

Primary CTA:
Book Your Home Massage

Secondary CTA:
Message on WhatsApp

Use prominent CTA buttons throughout the website.
